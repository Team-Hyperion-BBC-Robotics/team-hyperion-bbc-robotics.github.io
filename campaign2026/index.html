BACK FROM THE WORLD STAGE

Team Hyperion has returned from the 2026 RoboCup Junior World Championship in Incheon, South Korea — our second appearance on the international stage.

In Incheon we placed 12th individually and 4th in SuperTeam, improving on our individual ranking from Salvador 2025 while continuing to represent Brisbane Boys' College and Australia among the world's best junior soccer robotics teams.

A full campaign recap — match stories, photos, and what we learned — is on the way. For now, the results are in.
